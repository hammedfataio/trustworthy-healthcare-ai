## Current Research

### Experiment 001 — Baseline Medical Image Classification ✅

Established a reproducible PneumoniaMNIST baseline achieving **88.46% accuracy**
and **0.9370 AUROC**, while identifying substantial class-specific performance
differences and high-confidence errors.

### Experiment 002 — Confidence Calibration ✅

Investigated reliability diagrams, Brier score, ECE, high-confidence errors,
and temperature scaling. A validation-fitted temperature of **1.007948**
produced negligible calibration improvement, motivating deeper investigation
of predictive uncertainty.

### Experiment 003 — Uncertainty Quantification 🔬 Next

**Research question:** Can uncertainty estimates identify predictions that
should be treated cautiously or referred for human review?
